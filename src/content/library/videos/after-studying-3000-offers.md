Cole Gordon has scaled his own company past $30M/year and worked with 3,000+ business owners — this is the offer-building training he runs privately for high-level clients, stitched into one dense 60-minute video.

If you've read Hormozi's *$100M Offers* and felt like it was missing the "how do I actually implement this for my service business" layer, this is that layer. Cole's framing is sharper on the mechanics — the cost curve analysis, the 3S Formula, and the barbell positioning model are things I haven't seen articulated this cleanly elsewhere.
